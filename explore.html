Settled, open, and where this study stands

Settled by existing work

  • The rules: when an acquired business may be excluded, and for how long (SEC staff FAQ Q3; FRM 4310.11).
  • When a newly public company may omit management's report (FRM 4310.6).
  • How auditors treat the exclusion (PCAOB AS 2201).
  • That material weaknesses are common around IPOs, in aggregate (KPMG; Audit Analytics).
  • Who tends to use the acquisition exemption, in aggregate (Kravet, McVay & Weber, 2018).

Still open

  • Free records, company by company, linked to the filings.
  • How large excluded businesses are, case by case.
  • Whether each excluded business enters the next assessment.
  • One method that follows both transitions to the next assessment.
  • How long disclosed remediation takes, from checkable filings.
  • Practical steps traced to observed cases.

This study, today

  • Protocol v1.4 registered on OSF on October 31, 2026 (osf.io/jnpr2).
  • Pilot: 33 candidate filings screened; 12 company-events verified.
  • Search validated and 2025 screen run, November 1, 2026; fixed sample of 30 per transition published November 2, 2026, before coding.
  • Investigator coding of all 60 cases completed and locked November 8, 2026; independent second coding of 20 cases still to come.
  • Draft brief v1.1 and draft checklist v0.2 published for comment.
  • No findings published yet. The open questions are answered only when the main sample is coded, checked and released.

What existing work covers, and how large the gap is

Eleven sources were opened in a first-pass review (October 28, 2026). Each was checked against six points. The percentages below are computed from that grid; switch the counting rule to see how they change.

Coverage of each point, across 11 sources

What the percentages mean. 11 sources × 6 points = 66 checks. The large figure is the share of those checks with no coverage (the 2 checks the source does not document are counted as not covered). The figure that matters most for this study is the first bar in red: free, case-level data, which no reviewed source provides. These are measures of a first-pass review, not of all literature; paywalled handbooks, the full Audit Analytics data dictionary and full academic papers are still to be checked.
