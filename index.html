ICFR Transitions Project

A filing-based study of internal control over financial reporting (ICFR) at two points where a U.S. public company's annual assessment changes footing, and free practitioner guidance built from what the filings show.

The two transitions

Acquired operations

SEC staff allow management to exclude a recently acquired business from its ICFR assessment for up to one year. The study follows each excluded business to the next annual assessment.

Newly public companies

A newly public company may omit management's ICFR report from its first annual report. The study follows each company to its first full assessment and any remediation that follows.

Method in brief

All data comes from public SEC filings on EDGAR. Candidate filings are found with fixed full-text searches, screened by written rules, and confirmed by hand against Item 9A of each 10-K before they enter the dataset. Every record links to its source filing so anyone can check it.
