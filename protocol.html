Internal Control Readiness at Two Reporting Transitions: A Filing-Based Study of Acquired Operations and Newly Public Companies

Abstract

This study follows U.S. public companies through two points where management's annual assessment of internal control over financial reporting (ICFR) changes scope: (1) when a recently acquired business is excluded from the assessment, which SEC staff permit for no more than one year and one annual report, and is then brought into it; and (2) when a newly public company omits management's report under the SEC transition period and then makes its first assessment. Using public SEC filings only, it builds case histories linked to the filings at three levels: issuer-event, acquired business and individual weakness. It then develops free, evidence-based practitioner guidance on internal-control readiness and remediation, with technical review and a usability test. Existing work reports aggregate IPO material-weakness rates and studies the acquisition exemption in aggregate (Kravet, McVay and Weber, 2018). This study adds checkable case-level histories, acquisition-level follow-through, and guidance whose recommendations trace to those cases.

Research questions

No confirmatory hypotheses are tested. The study answers five descriptive questions for the search-identified, verified cohorts:

  • RQ1: how often a material weakness is disclosed at the transition and in the next annual assessment;
  • RQ2: which weakness categories recur, and whether weaknesses are attributed to the acquired business or the company as a whole;
  • RQ3: for acquisitions, how large each excluded business is and whether it clearly enters management's next assessment;
  • RQ4: how long disclosed remediation takes, from first observed disclosure;
  • RQ5: whether RQ1 and RQ2 differ with and without an auditor's ICFR attestation, as descriptive subgroups only.

No causal claims and no population-wide prevalence claims will be made.

Design in brief

Descriptive cohort study of 10-K filings dated in 2025 on SEC EDGAR, with follow-up to the next annual report filed on or before December 31, 2026. Candidate filings are found with frozen, validated full-text searches and fixed screening rules, and every candidate is verified by hand. A sample of up to 30 eligible issuer-events per cohort is fixed in a pre-set random order before coding, with no substitution. An independent second coder codes a random subset blind to the investigator's codes. Results are descriptive: counts with numerators and denominators, shares, medians and ranges. Target completion date: June 30, 2027.
