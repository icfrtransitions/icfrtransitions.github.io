Acquisition-to-Assessment Evidence Checklist

A draft checklist for bringing an acquired business into management's ICFR assessment: scope, owners, systems, evidence and remediation.

Draft for comment. This is a reasoned proposal drawn from SEC staff guidance and public disclosures. It has not yet been reviewed by independent practitioners or tested in practice. Under the project's protocol, it becomes a reviewed tool only after written technical review and a usability test.

Checklist

Basis: R regulatory requirement (cited in the brief) · J professional judgment. Items will be tagged O (observed pattern, with case IDs) once the pilot data is released.

AreaItemEvidence to keepBasis
1. Scope and timingAcquired entities, locations and processes identifiedEntity list; legal-entity chart; acquisition agreement scheduleR
Significance measured (assets and revenue), with the denominator statedCalculation tied to the consolidated amounts disclosed in Item 9AR
Acquisition date, first year-end and first included assessment date recorded separatelyTimeline showing the one-year limit and the one-report limitR
2. Process inventory and ownershipSignificant processes at the acquired business inventoriedProcess list mapped to significant accountsJ
An accountable control owner named for each process, on both sides of the dealOwner register with dates assignedJ
3. SystemsLedger and ERP migrations, interfaces and conversion reconciliations documentedConversion reconciliations; interface inventoryJ
User access and IT general controls in place for systems that remainAccess reviews; change-management evidenceJ
4. Financial reportingPurchase accounting and the opening balance sheet supportedValuation support; measurement-period adjustments logJ
Intercompany and consolidation reconciliations performedSigned reconciliations by periodJ
Close calendar aligned with the parent'sCombined close calendarJ
5. Evidence that controls operatedControls running long enough before year-end to be testedControl start dates versus year-endJ
Review evidence kept: who reviewed what, when, and what was followed upReview sign-offs and follow-up recordsJ
6. Exceptions and deficienciesA log of exceptions, with severity evaluated individually and in totalDeficiency log with aggregation analysisJ
7. RemediationStages tracked separately: planned, implemented, operating, tested, remediatedRemediation tracker with a date for each stageJ
8. DisclosureItem 9A names the excluded business and its significance; material changes in ICFR disclosedDraft Item 9A wording checked against SEC FAQ Question 3R
