## 1. First National Corp: eligibility decision

**Decided by the investigator on November 6, 2026: Eligible.**

- **Filing:** First National Corp /VA/, 10-K for fiscal year 2024 ([0001437749-25-010228](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/719402/000143774925010228/0001437749-25-010228-index.htm)), filed March 31, 2025. It is an original 10-K, filed outside the March 3–6 development window.
- **Statement.** It appears in Item 8, under the heading "MANAGEMENT'S REPORT REGARDING THE EFFECTIVENESS OF INTERNAL CONTROLS OVER FINANCIAL REPORTING", dated March 31, 2025: "As permitted by the guidance issued by the Office of the Chief Accountant of the Securities and Exchange Commission, management excluded the operations of Touchstone from its assessment of internal control over financial reporting as of December 31, 2024."

- **Rules.** The business is named in management's own report (C6); interpretation I1 is not needed. Item 9A says nothing about the exclusion. The report states that it was not subject to attestation by the auditor.
- **Read:** the investigator's decision rests on the passage above, which was read in the filing on November 6, 2026 (built-in browser, AI-assisted extraction).

**Consequences.**
- First National is AQ addition 32, a frame-completeness correction. The fixed sample is unchanged.
- It is an in-scope filing wrongly excluded by the 2025 screen, S2 and S3. Protocol section 7 therefore applies again.

## 2. Why the screens missed it (correction)

[Q3-validation-records-2026-11-06.md](Q3-validation-records-2026-11-06.md) gave as a likely reason that S3 lacks the term "merged". **That was wrong.**
- "excluded" is within 300 characters of "acquisition" ("the date of acquisition"), so the Q3 AQ test passes the passage.
- The cause is that no screen read the passage. S2's management's-report source starts only at "management's report on internal control" or "management's annual report on internal control". The heading here is "Management's Report Regarding the Effectiveness of Internal Controls". In the converted text the apostrophe is also separated by spaces ("MANAGEMENT ' S REPORT"), which S2's phrase does not allow.
- S3 reused S2's passages, so it missed the filing too.

## 4. Screen S4

**Scope.** The 4,442 AQ filings that the 2025 screen excluded under X3 and S2 did not pass. This is the S3 frame: SHA-256 `f544ac4c…`, see [S3-results-2026-11-04.md](S3-results-2026-11-04.md). NP is not re-screened: the transition statement belongs in Item 9A, which S2 already read in full.

**Passages (new source).** A passage starts at every match of either phrase, case-insensitive:
- "management" + optional apostrophe (straight, curly or backtick, with or without surrounding spaces) + optional "s" + optional "annual" + "report";
- "report of management".

It is kept only if "internal control" appears within 200 characters of its start. It ends at the first of:
- "Report of Independent Registered Public Accounting Firm";
- "Item 9B";
- 8,000 characters after the start.

Passages under 200 characters are ignored. The text conversion is the same as in Q3, S2 and S3.

**Test.** A passage passes S4 if it passes the Q3 AQ test: an exclusion term within 300 characters of an acquisition term, with Q3's term lists. A filing passes S4 if any passage passes.

**Not applied.** The S3 sentence test is not applied to the new passages. On management's-report text it matches auditor-attestation boilerplate in most filings (finding in S3-results).

**Review.** Filings that pass S4 go to eligibility review under the codebook (C6, I1, C10, C11). Passing the screen is not eligibility. Filings already decided keep their decisions. Eligible issuer-events are frame-completeness additions and do not enter the fixed sample.
