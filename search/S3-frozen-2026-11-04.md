## 1. Arcadium Lithium: eligibility decision

**Decided by the investigator on November 3, 2026 (Eastern time): Eligible.**

- **Filing:** Arcadium Lithium plc, 10-K for fiscal year 2024 ([0001977303-25-000006](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1977303/000197730325000006/0001977303-25-000006-index.htm)), filed February 27, 2025.
  - The filing date is outside the March 3–6 development window.
  - A 10-K/A filed March 17, 2025 does not change the anchor.
- **Statement.** Management's Annual Report on Internal Control over Financial Reporting (Item 8, incorporated in Item 9A) says: "On January 4, 2024, Arcadium Lithium closed on the Allkem Livent Merger, consolidating Allkem … Allkem's business has, therefore, been excluded from management's assessment of internal control over financial reporting for the year ended December 31, 2024."
- **Event.** Livent was the accounting acquirer. Allkem was acquired on January 4, 2024, in the fiscal year assessed. Allkem represented about 49.8% of total assets and 33.5% of revenue.
- **Rules:** named business (C6), excluded in management's own report; interpretation I1 is not needed.

**Consequences.**
- The first exclusion audit's AQ sample contained **2 wrongly excluded in-scope filings of 541** (Liberty Media and Arcadium Lithium), not 1. The first was found by the AI-assisted audit; the second only by the one-by-one re-check.
- Arcadium is an AQ issuer-event added as a frame-completeness correction, like the S2 additions. AQ additions now total **27** (26 from S2 and Arcadium). The fixed sample is unchanged (decision of November 3, 2026).
- Note for coding: Arcadium was acquired by Rio Tinto in March 2025, so later annual reports for the follow-up may not exist.

## 2. Section 7 trigger

Protocol section 7 says any wrongly excluded in-scope filing triggers a rule revision and a re-screen. Arcadium was excluded under X3 by the 2025 screen, and screen S2 ([S2-frozen-2026-11-03.md](S2-frozen-2026-11-03.md)) did not recover it.

**Why S2 missed it.** The nearest acquisition term ("Merger") is 394 characters before "excluded", beyond S2's 300-character window. Nothing after the sentence contains an acquisition term.

**Decision (investigator):** revise now. Screen S3 below is frozen and then run on the X3 exclusions that S2 did not pass.

## 3. Screen S3

**Scope.** AQ filings excluded under X3 in the 2025 screen that did not pass S2: **4,442 filings** (see [S2-results-2026-11-03.md](S2-results-2026-11-03.md)). NP is not re-screened: the one-by-one re-check of the first audit found no missed NP statement.

**Passages.** Exactly as in S2: every Item 9A passage (source 1) and every management's report passage (source 2). The Q3 text conversion is unchanged.

**Test.** A passage passes S3 if both of these hold (case-insensitive):
1. it contains at least one Q3 acquisition term anywhere: `acqui`, `merger`, `purchas`, `business combination`;
2. it contains a sentence of under 1,500 characters that has:
   - an exclusion word: `excluded`, `excludes`, `exclude`, `omitted`, `omits`, `did not include`, `does not include`, `do not include`, `not included`, `did not assess`, `does not extend`, `did not extend`, `outside the scope`, `outside of the scope` (whole words);
   - and a scope word: `assessment`, `evaluation`, `evaluated`, `assessed`, `internal control`.

Sentences are split at a period or semicolon followed by a space and a capital letter (or an opening quote or parenthesis).

A filing passes S3 if any passage passes. Filings that pass go to eligibility review under the codebook (with C6 and I1, as applied), the same way S2 passes did. Passing the screen is not eligibility.
