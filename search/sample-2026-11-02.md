# Verified sample: 2025 cohorts

ICFR Transitions Study. Protocol v1.4, section 5 step 4. Published before any coding begins.

## Method

- **Order:** each cohort's ordered candidate list ([AQ](candidates-AQ-2026-11-01.md), [NP](candidates-NP-2026-11-01.md); seed 20261103) was walked in rank order until 30 eligible issuer-events were reached.
- **First pass:** an AI-assisted first pass suggested a decision for each candidate, with reasons and the key sentence. It read EDGAR filing history and the primary document.
- **Decisions:** the investigator read each filing and made every decision. Where the decision differs from the first-pass suggestion, including every row the first pass left for judgment, it is marked *overruled*.
- **Unsure rows:** two rows were first marked Unsure and resolved the next day after a targeted read of the filing (AQ #16, NP #12). Both resolutions are recorded in the notes.
- **Audit additions:** AQ rank 330 (Liberty Media) and NP rank 119 (TechTarget) were not reached, because each list reached 30 eligible first.
- **Notes:** the investigator's notes are reproduced as recorded, so every candidate passed over has its reason.
