# Screen S2: eligibility decisions

ICFR Transitions Study. Registered protocol v1.4: https://osf.io/jnpr2. This completes the eligibility review of the 57 filings that passed screen S2 ([S2-results-2026-11-03.md](S2-results-2026-11-03.md)), under deviation D1 in [corrections-and-deviations-2026-11-03.md](corrections-and-deviations-2026-11-03.md).

**Decided by the investigator on November 3, 2026.** These filings cannot enter the fixed sample of 30 per cohort. Eligible ones are reported as a frame-completeness correction.

## How the decisions were made

1. **AI-assisted first pass** (Claude, through the project's automation): a suggestion, a reason and a key sentence for each filing.
2. **Second AI-assisted review**, commissioned by the investigator using a separate AI tool. It re-read all 57 filings and, for the likely positives, the prior annual report.
3. **Investigator decision.** The investigator checked the two reviews and adopted the decisions below, ruling on the two open cases (Casella and Olympic Steel).

Neither AI review is a human independent review. The second coder has not looked at these filings.

**Errors in the first pass, found by the second review and confirmed against the filings:**
- For Casella and Olympic Steel, the first pass quoted sentences from the auditor's report as if they were management's.
- For Boston Scientific, the first pass picked an unrelated sentence. Management's report names Silk Road Medical and Axonics.

## Rules applied

- **Naming rule:** clarification C6, as confirmed on November 3, 2026.
- **Interpretation I1 (new, applied to Casella).** The exclusion may be established by management's report together with the auditor's report, if the excluded businesses are named in management's report. In Casella's case, management's report names the businesses and their acquisition dates, its scope sentence is incomplete, and the auditor's report states that management excluded them.
  - I1 does not extend to naming: a business named only by the auditor does not meet C6. This is why Olympic Steel is not eligible.
  - I1 was adopted after these filings were read, so it is a clarification of the rule as applied. It will be applied the same way to all later decisions.
