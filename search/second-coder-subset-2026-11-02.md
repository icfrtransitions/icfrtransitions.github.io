## What happens next

- The investigator codes all 30 cases in each cohort, using the same procedure for every case.
- An independent second coder with ICFR experience codes these 10 cases per cohort without seeing the investigator's codes. They code the key variables: inclusion status, management and auditor scope, weakness presence, category, attribution and remediation stage.
- Raw agreement is reported for every variable. Cohen's kappa is added where section 7 allows it.
- Disagreements are reconciled after independent coding, and the reconciliation is logged.
