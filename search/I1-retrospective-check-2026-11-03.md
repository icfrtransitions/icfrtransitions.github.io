# Interpretation I1: retrospective check of earlier decisions

ICFR Transitions Study. Registered protocol v1.4: https://osf.io/jnpr2. Prepared November 3, 2026.

## Status of I1

[S2-eligibility-2026-11-03.md](S2-eligibility-2026-11-03.md) introduced interpretation I1:

> The exclusion may be established by management's report together with the auditor's report, if the excluded businesses are named in management's report.

I1 was adopted **after** the filings it was first applied to (Casella) had been read. It is a post-review interpretation, not a rule fixed before review. Calling it a clarification there does not change that. Accordingly:

- every earlier decision I1 could affect has been checked (below);
- results are reported both with and without I1 (see "Sensitivity").

## Which decisions I1 can affect

- I1 widens how an exclusion can be shown. It can only change **Not eligible** decisions to **Eligible**, and only where the reason was the absence of an exclusion statement by management. Eligible decisions cannot be affected.
- I1 does not change the naming rule (C6). A business named only by the auditor still does not qualify.
- I1 concerns acquired-business exclusions, so NP decisions are not affected.

## Scope of the check

All earlier AQ decisions of **Not eligible** for which the filings are identified in the public record:

- **Main sample:** the 13 AQ candidates decided Not eligible in the reading of ranks 1–43 ([sample-2026-11-02.md](sample-2026-11-02.md)): ranks 2, 6, 9, 10, 13, 16, 17, 21, 24, 27, 29, 32 and 35.
- **S2:** the 28 AQ filings decided Not eligible in [S2-eligibility-2026-11-03.md](S2-eligibility-2026-11-03.md).

**Method.** Each of the 41 filings was searched through the project's automation (AI-assisted, November 3, 2026) for any statement, by management or by the auditor, that management excluded something from its ICFR assessment. Every hit was read. A filing with no such statement anywhere cannot be affected by I1.

## Result

- **35 filings:** no statement by anyone that management excluded anything from its assessment. I1 cannot apply, and the decisions stand.
- **6 filings** contain exclusion wording. Each remains Not eligible for a reason I1 does not touch:

| Decision set | Company | Exclusion wording found | Reason it stays Not eligible |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main, AQ #16 | Sonida Senior Living | Management's sentence refers to "disclosure controls"; the auditor confirms that the ICFR assessment excluded the acquisitions | Under I1 the exclusion would be established, but the businesses are identified only by place-based labels (C6) |
| Main, AQ #27 | Pangaea Logistics Solutions | The auditor's report excludes Pangaea Baltimore LLC | 10-K/A for fiscal 2023, anchored outside the 2025 window |
| Main, AQ #32 | Camping World | Management excludes the "2024 Excluded Acquisition" | Described, not named (C6) |
| S2, AQ #14 | Trimble | Excludes "businesses acquired in 2023" | Not named (C6); 10-K/A for fiscal 2023 |
| S2, AQ #35 | Olympic Steel | Management excludes "the company it acquired during the fiscal 2024"; only the auditor names Metal Works, LLC | Named only by the auditor (C6); I1 does not extend to naming |
| S2, AQ #53 | Arthur J. Gallagher & Co. | Excludes "22 of the 48 entities acquired in 2024" | The excluded entities are not identified (C6) |

**No decision changes.** The fixed sample and the S2 counts are unchanged.

## Limits

The search was automated and AI-assisted. A statement worded in a way the search did not cover could have been missed. The six hits were read in the filings. This check is not a human independent review.
