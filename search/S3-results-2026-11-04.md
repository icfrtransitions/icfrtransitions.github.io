## The rule is too broad (finding about S3)

Most passes are false matches. Smaller companies' Item 9A often says, for example, "This annual report does not include an attestation report of our registered public accounting firm …". That sentence meets S3's sentence test (an exclusion word plus "internal control"). The passage passes whenever the word "acquisition" or "merger" appears anywhere in the 20,000-character passage.

None of the nine development filings showed this. The frozen rule is reported as run and is not changed after the fact.

## Triage of the 505 passes (AI-assisted; not part of the frozen rule)

To find the real exclusion statements, each passing filing's S3 passages were searched (November 4, 2026, AI-assisted) for every sentence that:
- has an exclusion word and a scope word, as in S3;
- is not auditor-attestation or framework boilerplate (attestation, emerging growth company, smaller reporting company, COSO and similar wording).

- **490 filings:** no such sentence. These are recorded as **Not eligible: no exclusion statement (AI triage)**. They were not read individually by the investigator.
- **15 filings** had a sentence. Of these:

  - 7 were not acquisition exclusions and are recorded as **Not eligible (AI triage)**:

    - Kimball Electronics: auditor's revenue procedures.
