- **Consistency check.** For each filing, the run also re-applied the Q3 test to the Q3 Item 9A passage. No filing passed it, which agrees with the original X3 decisions.

**What this means.** The S2 test is a text-proximity screen, so many passes will be false positives. In 25 of the 55 AQ passes, the first matched text is clearly not an exclusion statement (for example, "excluding acquisitions" in a cash-flow statement). A real statement may still appear elsewhere in the same passage, so every pass goes to eligibility review.

In the other 30 AQ passes, the matched text reads as a management statement excluding a named or described acquired business. If even part of these prove eligible, the 2025 AQ frame missed substantially more in-scope filings than the first exclusion audit (1 of 541) suggested. That finding will be reported whatever the eligibility review shows.

**Observation.** ANI Pharmaceuticals (0001023024-25-000015), the one Q3 validation miss (clarification C7), passes S2 on source 2. Its management's report says the assessment "did not include the internal controls of Alimera Sciences, Inc." S2 was not designed for this case.
