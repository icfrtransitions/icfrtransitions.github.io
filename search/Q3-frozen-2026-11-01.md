ICFR Transitions Study. Registered protocol v1.4: https://osf.io/jnpr2 (DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/JNPR2). Supersedes [Q2](Q2-frozen-2026-11-01.md) for both cohorts.

**Frozen on November 1, 2026, before either Q3 validation sample was drawn.** This file's GitHub commit date is the public timestamp. Nothing in it changes after commit. If recall in either cohort is below 80%, a further set (Q4) is written from development cases only, frozen in a new file, and validated on another new sample.

## Why Q2 was revised

Q2 found 7 of 15 eligible AQ positives (47%) in the first validation sample ([results](AQ-validation-results-2026-11-01.md)), below the 80% threshold in protocol section 4. Q2 is unchanged and stays on record.

The cause is structural: exact-phrase searches only find the exact wording given, and EDGAR full-text search does not match word variants (a search for "excluded" does not find "exclude"). Q3 therefore uses broad searches with wildcards, which return most 10-Ks, and moves the selection into an automated Item 9A screen with fixed rules.

The NP queries are revised at the same time, before any NP validation sample has been drawn, because they share the same exact-phrase weakness. In development, Q2's NP2 missed one of four NP positives (Ingram Micro). The NP validation below tests Q3. Q2's NP result on the same sample is also reported, for comparison.

**Disclosure.** The investigator and the AI assistant saw the wording of the eight Q2 misses in the first AQ sample. Those cases were not used to write or test Q3. Q3 was written and scored on the development cases listed below. The protection against tuning to the test is that both Q3 samples are new and drawn only after this file is committed.

## Screen

Rules X1, X2 and X4 of protocol section 6 are unchanged. Rules R0, X3, AQ-IN and NP-IN are implemented as the following automated tests:

1. **Item 9A text.** The filing's main document is converted to text. Item 9A is the longest passage that starts at "Item 9A" and ends at the next "Item 9B" (or 20,000 characters if none), ignoring any passage over 60,000 characters (the table of contents is the shorter passage).
2. **R0.** If Item 9A is under 500 characters or not found, the filing goes to manual review.
3. **AQ test (X3 / AQ-IN).** Pass if Item 9A contains an exclusion term within 300 characters of an acquisition term. Exclusion terms: `exclu`, `omit`, `did not includ`, `does not includ`, `not includ`, `not extend`, `did not assess`, `not assess`, `outside the scope`, `outside of the scope`. Acquisition terms: `acqui`, `merger`, `purchas`, `business combination`. Case-insensitive.
4. **NP test (X3 / NP-IN).** Pass if Item 9A contains `newly public`, `newly-public` or `transition period` within 300 characters of `not includ`, `does not contain`, `not required`, `omit` or `exempt`. Case-insensitive.
5. Filings that pass, and R0 filings, go to manual eligibility review under the codebook. Passing the screen is not eligibility.

## Development results (frozen before any Q3 sampling)

Development cases are defined by protocol section 4: Pilot A, Pilot B and filings in the development window (annual reports filed March 3–6, 2025).

**AQ.** All 1,004 acquisitions in the 2024 Item 2.01 frame were checked for whose next 10-K fell in the development window, and those 10-Ks were read. Eleven exclude an acquired business: Ambac, Kinetik, California Resources, PAR Technology, SS&C, Hilton Grand Vacations, Nuvation Bio, Myers Industries, Forum Energy Technologies, Innovex International and Solaris Energy Infrastructure. Q3 retrieved 11 of 11 and the screen passed 11 of 11. None of the other development-window acquirers had a sentence-level match in Item 9A.

**NP.** The 2024 IPO frame (below) has five operating companies whose first 10-K fell in the development window: Concentra, Contineum, Ingram Micro, LandBridge and Silvaco. Four are positives. Q3 retrieved 4 of 4 and the screen passed 4 of 4. Silvaco gave a full management assessment, and the screen correctly did not pass it.

In the development window, AQ3-1 alone returned about 400 10-K documents. Q3 is therefore close to a census of 10-Ks, with the screen doing the selection.

## Q3 validation sampling (fixed now, before drawing)

**AQ, second sample.**

- **Frame:** the same 1,004 Form 8-K filings dated 2024 reporting Item 2.01 (SHA-256 of the seed-20261101 order: `eb7422ec8a6a1c0c0b42e569342cfada52a4f0446d79eca3b7b0abfe9b5dc4f8`).
- **Removed before drawing:**
  - positions 1–100 of the first seed order (the first sample and the cases seen after it);
  - every filing whose acquirer's next 10-K was filed March 3–6, 2025 (development window).
- **Order:** the remaining filings, sorted by accession number, are shuffled with mulberry32 seed **20261102** (Fisher–Yates).
- **Reading:** in that order, keep a filing only if it reports a completed acquisition of a business by a registrant that files annual reports on Form 10-K. Read the acquirer's next 10-K, meaning the first 10-K filed after the 8-K with a fiscal period ending in or after the 8-K month. Record whether management excluded the acquired business from its ICFR assessment (an eligible positive).
- **Stop:** at 15 eligible positives or 100 acquisitions read.

**NP sample.**

- **Frame:** Form 424B4 filings dated 2024 returned by EDGAR full-text search for "initial public offering". The earliest per company is kept: 318 companies.
- **Order:** sorted by accession number and shuffled with mulberry32 seed **20261101**, as fixed in the Q2 file. The NP sample has not been drawn before.
- **Keep a company only if all of these hold:**
  - it filed no 10-K, 10-Q, 20-F or 40-F before the prospectus date;
  - its SIC code is not 6770, and its first 10-K does not describe it as a "blank check company";
  - it is not a fund, trust or BDC;
  - it filed a 10-K after the prospectus;
  - that first 10-K was not filed March 3–6, 2025.
- **Draw:** the first 20 kept companies are the sample.
- **Reading:** read each one's first 10-K and record whether Item 9A omits management's assessment under the newly public transition period (an eligible positive).

**Recall.** For each cohort, recall is the share of eligible positives whose 10-K is returned by a Q3 query and passes the screen (or goes to R0 review). The query check uses EDGAR full-text search restricted to the company and the calendar year of filing. Recall must be at least 80% in each cohort: 12 of 15 for AQ, and 80% of the NP positives, whatever their number.

Every drawn item, its decision and the reason are published with the results. Eligibility decisions are first-pass, AI-assisted, and verified by the investigator.
