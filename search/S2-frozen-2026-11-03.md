# Screen S2: frozen rule

ICFR Transitions Study. Registered protocol v1.4: https://osf.io/jnpr2. This file carries out deviation D1 in [corrections-and-deviations-2026-11-03.md](corrections-and-deviations-2026-11-03.md).

**Committed on November 3, 2026, before S2 is run on the 2025 frame.** This file's GitHub commit date is the public timestamp. S2 has been run on two filings only: the two wrongful exclusions found by the first audit (see "Development" below).

## Scope

S2 is applied only to filings that the 2025 screen excluded under rule X3: 4,497 AQ and 4,722 NP. It does not re-test filings excluded under X1, X2 or X4, and it does not change any decision already published.

## Rule

S2 keeps every step of the Q3 screen ([Q3-frozen-2026-11-01.md](Q3-frozen-2026-11-01.md)) and adds two text sources. A filing excluded under X3 is re-tested on each source below, using the Q3 AQ test or the Q3 NP test for its cohort, with the same terms and the same 300-character proximity:

1. **Every Item 9A passage.** Each passage that starts at "Item 9A" and ends at the next "Item 9B" (or 20,000 characters if none), ignoring any passage over 60,000 characters. Q3 tested only the longest such passage.
2. **Management's report passages.** Each passage that starts at a phrase "management's report on internal control" or "management's annual report on internal control" (case-insensitive; straight or curly apostrophe), wherever it appears in the main document, and ends at the first of: "Report of Independent Registered Public Accounting Firm", "Item 9B", or 8,000 characters after the start. Passages under 200 characters are ignored.

A filing passes S2 if any passage from either source passes the test for its cohort. Otherwise it stays excluded under X3.

Filings that pass S2 go to manual eligibility review under the codebook, as Q3 passes did. Passing the screen is not eligibility.

## What S2 does not change

- The Q3 search terms are not widened. The ANI Pharmaceuticals miss ("except for" wording, clarification C7) is not addressed by S2.
- R0 (manual review of filings with no usable Item 9A) is unchanged.

## Development (two cases; disclosed)

Before freezing, a draft of this rule was run on the two filings that prompted it, through the project's automation on November 3, 2026. The draft had only source 2, worded as a heading. What the run showed:

| Case | Why Q3 missed it (as reproduced) | S2 result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Liberty Media Corp (AQ, 0001558370-25-001830) | Item 9A (about 1,700 characters) cross-references management's report, which sits elsewhere in the document. The report says the assessment "did not include the internal controls of Quint Events, LLC which the Company acquired in the first quarter of 2024". | Passes on source 2 |
| TechTarget, Inc. (NP, 0000950170-25-078416) | The document has no "Item 9B" heading after Item 9A, so passages are capped at 20,000 characters. A cross-reference to Item 9A in the risk factors gives a passage of equal length, and that passage was taken instead of the real Item 9A. The transition statement is in the real Item 9A. | Passes on source 2; would also pass on source 1 |

Changes made after the run:
- source 1 was added, for the TechTarget cause;
- "heading" became "phrase", because the text conversion does not keep headings.

These two cases informed the rule, so their passing is a check, not validation. Any filing that the by-hand audit (D2) finds wrongly excluded and that S2 does not pass will be reported, with the reason.

## Handling additions (decision of November 3, 2026)

- The fixed sample of 30 per cohort is kept.
- Eligible issuer-events added by S2 are listed with their count and a description, and compared descriptively with the sample on the main fields. They are reported as a frame-completeness correction.
- They are not placed into the random order and do not replace sample cases.
